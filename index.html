Transformers

Transformers are deep learning models designed for processing sequential data, especially in NLP tasks.
They use self-attention mechanisms to understand context and relationships in text.

Transformers power models like BERT and GPT, enabling advanced AI applications. Their efficiency has revolutionized machine learning and AI research.
