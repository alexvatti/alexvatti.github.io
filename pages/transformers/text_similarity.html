🔍 What is Text Similarity?

Text similarity is a method of measuring how semantically close two sentences are. Using the all-MiniLM-L6-v2 model, we can convert sentences into numerical vectors and compare them using cosine similarity. A score close to 1.0 means the sentences are very similar, while a score near 0.0 means they are unrelated.

How It Works

  • Enter a source sentence.
  • Enter multiple comparison sentences (one per line).
  • Click the "Check Similarity" button.
  • The app will compute a similarity score for each sentence.

Example Similarity Scores

Source Sentence: "I love programming."

  • "I enjoy coding." → 0.89 (High similarity)
  • "The weather is nice today." → 0.12 (Low similarity)
  • "I like writing software." → 0.85 (High similarity)
